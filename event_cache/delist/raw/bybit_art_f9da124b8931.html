Delisting of VANRY on Aug 18, 2026

Aug 11, 2026
Aug 11, 2026

Bybit is committed to providing a secure, high-quality and reliable trading environment for our community. In accordance with the Bybit Token Management Rules, we conduct regular reviews of all listed tokens and projects to ensure they continue to meet our standards for performance and compliance.

Based on our latest review, we have decided to delist and discontinue all trading services for the following token:

Delisted Token

Vanar (VANRY)

Delisted Trading Pair

VANRY/USDT

Please note that Bybit will not support the Vanar (VANRY) contract migration.

If you hold VANRY tokens and wish to participate in the migration, you will need to complete the token swap manually through Vanar Chain's official portal.

VANRY withdrawals via the Ethereum (ERC20) network will remain available, allowing you to transfer your tokens for manual migration.

For more information about the contract migration, please refer to Vanar Chain's official announcement.

Delisting Timeline

1. Spot

Trading of VANRY/USDT on Bybit's Spot platform will end after Aug 18, 2026, 8AM UTC. All open orders will be automatically removed.

2. Convert

VANRY will be removed from Bybit Convert after Aug 18, 2026, 8AM UTC.

3. Deposits

Deposits of VANRY will close after Aug 17, 2026, 8AM UTC.

4. Withdrawals

You may continue to withdraw your VANRY assets after the trading pair is delisted, as withdrawals will remain available.

However, we recommend withdrawing your assets as soon as possible to better manage them.

As Vanar (VANRY) undergoes its contract migration, please refer to the project's official announcement to complete the token swap manually. Bybit will not perform the token migration on behalf of users.

5. DCA Bot & Spot Grid Bot

If you have any active Spot Grid Bots or DCA Bots on VANRY/USDT, please close them before Aug 18, 2026, 8AM UTC. Otherwise, they will be automatically terminated by the system.

If you have any questions, please contact our Customer Support.

Thank you for your continued support.
