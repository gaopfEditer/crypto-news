Bybit to Delist 5 Token(s) on Nov 27, 2026

Nov 20, 2026
Nov 20, 2026

Bybit is committed to maintaining a secure and reliable trading environment for our community. In line with the Bybit Token Management Rules, we conduct regular reviews of all listed tokens and projects to make sure they meet our performance and compliance standards.

Following our latest review, we will delist and end trading for the following tokens.

Delisted tokens: DIAM,MBX,MCRT,NS,OBT

Delisted trading pairs: DIAMUSDT,MBXUSDT,MCRTUSDT,NSUSDT,OBTUSDT

Delisting timeline

Spot: Trading of DIAMUSDT,MBXUSDT,MCRTUSDT,NSUSDT,OBTUSDT pairs on Bybit’s Spot platform will end after Nov 27, 2026, 8:00AM UTC, and all open orders will be automatically removed.

Convert: DIAM,MBX,MCRT,NS,OBT tokens will be removed from Bybit Convert after Nov 27, 2026, 8:00AM UTC.

Deposits: Deposits for DIAM,MBX,MCRT,NS,OBT will close after Nov 26, 2026, 8:00AM UTC .

DCA Bot & Spot Grid Bot: If you have active Spot Grid Bots or DCA Bots on DIAMUSDT,MBXUSDT,MCRTUSDT,NSUSDT,OBTUSDT pairs, please close them beforeNov 27, 2026, 8:00AM UTC . Otherwise, they will be automatically terminated by the system.

Withdrawals: You can continue to withdraw your assets after the MBX,MCRT,NS,OBT trading pairs are closed, but we recommend doing so early to manage your assets effectively.

Asset retirement: Withdrawals for DIAM will no longer be available after 2027-01-26 08:00:00UTC. Once the withdrawal function closes, DIAM tokens may be converted into stablecoins on your behalf, though this is not guaranteed. Any converted stablecoins will be credited to your Bybit account.

If you have any questions, please contact our Customer Support.

Thank you for your continued support.
